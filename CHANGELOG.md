## v1.0.0 - 2026-10-15

- Added: SeneaL UI's features, SeneaL Radar's (with alerts and an ignore list) and SeneaL Recycle's run up to SeneaL UI's own maximums on servers that do not run SeneaL UI, set on a settings page in SeneaL UI's style with per-server switches and presets and a Hotkeys section, while a SeneaL UI server keeps every rule it states and a server running this mod decides which of its features players may use.
- Added: inventory tools: sort by value, chest auto-sort, restock to set amounts, chest cycling with Page Down and Page Up, multi-select, four loadouts, auto shield and shield pairs, cosmetic equipment, item comparison on hover, mouse wheel splitting, a portal stash button and an auto-pickup ignore list.
- Added: reminders for durability, food and timed effects with optional auto-eat, a Tracker of recipes, upgrades, trophies, pieces, skills, kills and items with the materials still needed, a food planner, a Death Report, a Progress card, a ping and list of items SeneaL UI drops when the inventory shrinks, and a character backup the first time each server is unlocked.
- Added: map pin search and bulk edit, pin sizes and looks carried through cartography tables, auto-pins with map chips to show or hide them, live pins for portals, ships, carts and tames, and range rings.
- Added: a party with a Party HUD in SeneaL UI's style and a shared map, and chat filters, colors, timestamps, mentions, friends, party and direct messages, hidden arrival text and a placed spot for off-screen chat.
- Added: HUD editor additions: lock, line-up and spacing tools in SeneaL UI's Objects list (an Elements panel where the editor has no Objects list), copying an element's look, growing from a corner, combat-only elements, layouts that keep their size at every resolution and on wide screens, and the built-in "SeneaL UI Unlocker" interface preset.
- Added: Build Restrictions, a build camera, Repair nearby, Undo Remove, Auto Doors, tame details and commands, production alerts and renaming a linked portal's partner with it, plus previews of station, ward, base and warmth areas and comfort level while a hammer is in hand, and extension ranges and plant spacing while placing.
- Added: the /find and /recipe chat commands, pins for what you look at, signs, dropped items and old beds, a hotbar swap key, chest, ship and cart names, and base status, skill progress and Sheltered and Comfort cards.
- Added: switches to turn off camera shake, the damage flash and fire smoke, an optional fuel helper and tool swap, a notice when a newer version is out, and cheats, whose switches are off by default.
- Fixed: SeneaL UI's mod settings open on the main menu too, with its key or a Mod Settings button, a preset without this mod's elements leaves their attachments as they are, the Crafting window can keep separate placements beside the inventory and at stations, and each HUD editor frame outlines exactly what its element draws.
- Changed: SeneaL UI's HUD editor settings leave its settings page, other mods' colored item names show their colors, its preview watermark can be hidden and its fixed keys are settings in your own world and on servers without it.
- Added: Input Tweaks 1.1.7, Valheim Terrain Tools 0.6.4, SmartCraftStorage 0.12.1, MultiCraft 1.5.0, DadsFPP 1.1.5, DadsSkills 1.0.0 and Badgers Shading Overhaul 0.6.0 work beside it, and the mod is tested on Valheim 1.0.20 with SeneaL UI 1.1.7, 1.1.8, 1.1.9, 1.2.0, 1.2.1, 1.2.2, 1.2.3, 1.2.4 and 1.2.5.
